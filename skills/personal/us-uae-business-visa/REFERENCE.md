### Third-party trust funding — U.S.

A revocable trust can be presented as a third-party funding source only when legally authorized. Treat it as sponsor/funder evidence, not as the applicant's personal asset unless that is legally true.

Recommended evidence:

- certification of trust or narrowly redacted trust excerpt;
- evidence of current trustee authority;
- trustee authorization letter identifying traveler and permitted expenses;
- recent bank letter or statement in the trust's legal name;
- direct-pay evidence, if the trust pays airfare, lodging, conference fees, legal fees, or other expenses directly.

Do not represent trust assets as belonging to the traveler. If fiduciary authority is unclear, require review by trust counsel.

### Critical routing rule

Do not combine GDRFA and ICP requirements. Determine the actual filing authority and visa route first.

### Financial-evidence rule

- Never invent a fixed minimum bank balance where the selected official service does not publish one.
- If the selected route requires a stamped six-month applicant bank statement, a trust bank statement does not replace it unless the authority expressly accepts a substitute.
- A third-party revocable trust may supplement the file as proof that travel, legal, incorporation, or related expenses will be paid, provided the trustee has legal authority to make those expenditures.
- A trust sponsorship letter does not automatically satisfy a separate "proof of investment" requirement.

### Third-party trust funding — UAE

Recommended package when a trust is part of the financial case:

1. Trustee authorization letter.
2. Certification of trust or redacted excerpt showing trustee authority.
3. Current bank letter/statement in the trust's legal name.
4. Evidence that the authorized trustee controls the account.
5. Clear expense scope: airfare, hotel, UAE legal fees, DIFC incorporation fees, or other specifically authorized costs.
6. Trust-counsel confirmation if the expenditure could raise beneficiary, fiduciary-duty, or trust-purpose questions.

For the current case, the **June P. Huggins Revocable Trust** may be used as supplemental financial evidence only if its trustee is legally authorized to fund the relevant expenses. Keep the trust's account numbers, balances, settlor/beneficiary details, and full trust instrument out of the public GitHub repository.

## Recommended funding architecture

Keep each funding source separate:

| Funding source | What it can prove | What it does not automatically prove |
|---|---|---|
| Applicant funds | Personal ability to pay | Organizational/trust authority |
| CED funds | Organizational sponsorship when authorized | Applicant's personal wealth |
| Revocable trust funds | Third-party financial support when trustee is authorized | Applicant ownership, return ties, or waiver of applicant-bank-statement requirements |

## Consistency audit

Before filing either visa case, compare forms and letters for:

- legal name and passport details;
- nationality and residence;
- job title and organization;
- CED status wording when material;
- charitable donation vs. securities investment distinctions;
- ownership statements;
- trip dates and length;
- destination and purpose;
- source of travel funds;
- exact identity of any third-party funder;
- trustee authority and permitted expense scope when trust funds are used;
- prior travel and visa history;
- employment vs. temporary business activity.
